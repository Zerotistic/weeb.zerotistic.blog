# Artwork and fonts

The Artoria reading and waving illustrations, sleeping black cat, favicon, and IBM Plex Sans/Mono fonts are reused from the owner's adjacent `zerotistic.blog` project. The reading illustration retains its original file. The cat is resized to 300px and encoded as WebP for display. The blog's `src/assets/about/README.md` documents the generated decorative cat; this is not a photograph of the owner's pet. IBM Plex is distributed under the SIL Open Font License; see `fonts/OFL.txt`.

Cover artwork is identification imagery from AniList, associated with the catalogue links in `collection.js`. Copyright remains with the respective creators and publishers. Larger versions of the blog's existing cover thumbnails were downloaded on 2026-09-25:

- Fate/stay night: https://s4.anilist.co/file/anilistcdn/media/anime/cover/large/bx356-mTpMvtillumS.png
- FLCL: https://s4.anilist.co/file/anilistcdn/media/anime/cover/large/bx227-qOB9ZhVvNnqO.jpg
- Plastic Memories: https://s4.anilist.co/file/anilistcdn/media/anime/cover/large/bx20872-j5PBzzVtrYDM.jpg
- Three Days of Happiness: https://s4.anilist.co/file/anilistcdn/media/manga/cover/large/bx97553-vqJj5DFS8v2p.jpg
- Parasite in Love: https://s4.anilist.co/file/anilistcdn/media/manga/cover/large/nx105768-Vh1iegsU5tJq.jpg

All assets are local; the page does not make third-party image or font requests.

The day and dusk Avalon landscape WebP files are copied unchanged from the main blog’s `public/static/avalon-landscape.webp` and `avalon-landscape-dusk.webp`. CSS fades them into the background without modifying the source artwork.

## Full inventory artwork

Cover and product sources for the real inventory are listed in `../data/catalog-sources.json`. Downloads are resized and encoded to WebP for display, without cropping or changing the artwork. These are catalogue images, not photographs of the owner’s copies; copyright remains with the respective creators, publishers and manufacturers. Flags indicate the owner’s edition language, not the language on the catalogue thumbnail. Missing or ambiguous product images are left unset.
